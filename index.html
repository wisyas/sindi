About Our Company

SINDI Advertising is a marketing agency from the Philippines which assists brands in developing distinct identities and in establishing enduring links with their intended audiences. Since the word 'sindi' means 'to ignite', we believe that excellent campaigns start with a spark. An idea that has the ability to capture attention and produce impact. We offer specialized services in the area of strategic campaign development, creative design, and complete communication solutions that are tailored to enhance your brand.

Our Story
2018
The Humble Beginning

SINDI was founded in a humble studio by a small, passionate team of five Filipino creatives. Bound by a shared conviction that local storytelling could compete on a global scale, we began taking on small creative projects for local startup brands.

2020
Resiliency Through Adaptation

Amid global uncertainties, the pandemic and etc we helped small to medium enterprises shift into digital-first communication channels, proving that meaningful storytelling can bridge gaps even during challenging times.

2022
Scaling the Flame

As demand grew, SINDI expanded into a full service creative and strategic agency, bringing on board data analysts, media planners, and multimedia producers.

2026
Continual Impact

Today, SINDI continues to spark purpose narratives. Eight years in, our passion remains unchanged turning bold ideas into measurable, high-impact movements across digital and traditional spaces.

2025 Initiative

The "Lokal Loud" Campaign

Led a multi channel campaign highlighting local craftsmanship across various provinces. The initiative combined experiential pop-ups, influencer collaborations, and nationwide outdoor displays, earning media coverage across major news outlets and driving over 5 Million video views.

2023 Campaign

Modernization × Rebranding

Partnered with a legacy commercial enterprise transitioning from traditional media to a contemporary digital identity. SINDI revitalized their visual branding and messaging architecture, resulting in a 45% increase in brand recall and a 25% jump in direct sales inquiries within the first quarter post-launch.

2021 Movement

Project Spark

Developed an integrated digital campaign for a home grown Philippine retail brand aiming to reconnect with Gen Z consumers. By leverage culturally resonant video storytelling and targeted social strategies, the campaign generated 3.2 Million organic impressions and boosted client engagement by 140% in under six months.
